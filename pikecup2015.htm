Pike Cup 2015

Home

Two days of fierce and nail-biting fishing resulted in a shock loss for the long time champion, Richard. With only half an hour to go it was looking close at one pike apiece, when Chris angled two pike to emerge triumphant.

 

Jonathan set the pace in the warm-up phase, trailblazing with an impressive specimen taken on a wobbly-jelly plug. The Cup was presented at a brief handover later that day.

 

The Sunday session was the 40th anniversary of Chris’s first pike. Mother’s diary entry for Saturday 15 Feb 1975: “Chris caught 3 pike”, the first of these weighing 2lb 12ozs. Potential entry for 15 Feb 2015 also: “Chris caught 3 pike”!

 

HAG/VAG members later had a celebratory lunch at Orton Hall Hotel. Then Rit and Chris made a dash to Peterborough train station.
