Members London reunion
by our special correspondent, Kensington
Members of the Orton Longueville Village Anglers Guild (VAG) met recently in London in celebration of 20 years of guild activities. Attendees recounted some amazing tall, but true, stories of fake pike, corpulent carp and sparkling spinners. Guild President, Mr. C. P. Firth, gave a stimulating presentation of vintage pike photos.

Co-founder, Mr. R. A. Barnes, as usual, tried heroically to match this embarrassment of old snaps and reportedly made the audience squirm just as acutely.

VAG Biography
by our research staff

The Early Years

Summer1974
CPF & RAB first fishing trip to Gudgeon Spot and Pond. "I Shot the Sheriff" a hit.
Autumn1974
VAG founded in 21a, The Village
Winter 1974/5
First trips to the Carp Hole. RAB catches first ever pike. CPF catches three in one day, first ever pike was 2lb 12ozs, largest of the season 5lb 6ozs.
Summer 1975
First trips to Alwalton. "Amateur Hour", "Now I'm Here" "This Town Aint..." chart.

The Golden Age

Winter 1975/76
CPF catches 11 and 12 pounders at Sibson. RAB catches the biggest in the Nene for the season, a 6lb pike. "Bohemian Rhapsody" at No.1 for nine weeks. CPF breaks collar bone and then elbow.
Summer 1976
The year of the classic "drought" summer with multiple "huge" chub trapped by pungent red cheddar bait.
Winter 1976/77
The year of small pike and the (re-)discovery of the Chub Spot using spinners.
Summer 1977
Multiple one-pound chub during another chub summer. RAB's last school holiday in Orton. CPF catches a 5lb carp on a Big S!

Fishing by Holidays, Trips and Visits

Winter 1977/78
CPF creates the all time record of 68 pike in one season. CPF finishes the season with a 9lb 8oz pike from the Pike Spot. RAB back at New Year.
Summer 1978
RAB returns to Orton to nab the biggest chub at the Big Chub Spot. Then CPF & RAB travel to Southwold where Waybread and Henham serve up record tench and crucian carp. Year of the tare for roach on the Nene.
Winter 1978/9
Poor pike season, RAB visits at Xmas and still catches a few.
Summer 1979
Second trip to Southwold. No records set!
Winter 1979/80
CPF down to 8 pike for the year (A Levels loom).
Summer 1980
Final trip to Southwold, time on the River Waveney marks Rit's last ever pike [actually not!]. CPF resumes the big hauls from the Pike Spot.

Going Solo

Winter 1980/81
CPF manages 43 for the season and the record pike of all time of 13¼lb from a becalmed Pike Spot. His last real season in Peterborough. JWB joins the VAG.
Winter 1981/82
Two pike for CPF during college break.
Winter 1982/3
The seventh pike of this winter marks the last ever pike for CPF and the VAG?   [No!]
Summer 1984
One last trip in Canada yields a 2½lb trout caught from a boat on Lake Minnewanka in the glory of the Alberta Rocky Mountains. The trout is cooked and eaten in style in a local restaurant!

SOME STATISTICS
CPF caught 177 pike of which 19 were over 4lbs and 3 were over 10lbs. CPF has caught 13 Chub, 1 bream, 2 eels and 1 trout over 1lb. RAB was the first to catch a pike. The most successful lure of all time was the Ondex, but livebait was the most profitable method. Members of VAG were CPF, RAB, "Southey", Stephen Hancock. (JWB) Jonathan Boughton was appointed full member in 2009.
