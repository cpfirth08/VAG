Rules

  • Daily points are awarded for total weight for the day: first place: 1 point; second place: 2 points; third place: 3 points.
  • Only pike count in the weight (so no trout, crab, perch or chub).
  • In case of a tie on the day, the tied anglers take the average points. For example: 2 anglers tie for 2nd place: 2+3 / 2 = 2.5 points each.
  • Anglers who do not catch fish on a day receive 1 penalty point.
  • The angler with the lowest combined points total after all days is the winner. In case of a tie, the total weight will be used to determine the winner.
