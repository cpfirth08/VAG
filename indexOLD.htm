Village Anglers Guild

The Village Anglers Guild (VAG) was founded in summer of 1974 in The Village, Orton Longueville to further the fishing exploits of the guild members. At various times members of VAG were Chris, Rit, "Southey" (Andrew Southern), Stephen Hancock and Jonathan Boughton. The VAG disbanded active angling in 1984 until it sprang back into life during Christmas 2000.
