Village Anglers Guild

The Village Anglers Guild (VAG) was founded in summer of 1974 in the village of Orton Longueville with the purpose of furthering the fishing exploits of the guild members. At various times members of VAG were Chris, Rit, "Southey" (Andrew Southern), Stephen Hancock and the later honorary member, Jonathan Boughton. The VAG effectively disbanded active angling in 1984.

This Web site is dedicated to the VAG, and it documents highlights of the fishing, fishes, locations (known as "spots") and personalities that made up the world of the VAG. The gudgeon above is an appropriate talisman for this club.
