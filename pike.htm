Observations of the Pike; with directions how to fish for him

by Chris Firth, written in Autumn 1979 (transcription in progress)

The Opposition: "The mighty Luce, or Pike, is taken to be the tyrant (as the salmon is the king) of the fresh waters". This is how Izaak Walton described the pike in 1653. A somewhat romantic view of a fish which, granted, is a greedy and savage predator but is alos a confirmed scavenger.Indeed the larger fish probably procure most of their nourushment from dead or sickly and injured fish which are close to expiring anyway. Although it could be claimed that the pike act as an ecological cleaning system, there is no doubt in my mind that pike will kill an enormous amount of healthy, adult fish.

The Technique: Livebaiting. The hunt for small fish is the biggest obstacle to a pike fishermen. That is to say, little bait fish can often not be caught on a day when the pike are feeding at their most ravenously frantic. A six inch livebait is about the maximum size of the rig I have described, and it is tru to say that this size livebait will catch any weight of pike. The livebait, in order of superiority are Perch, Dace, Gudgeon, Chub, Roach and Bleak.

The livebait should be hooked through the top lip, at least 5mm from the edge of the mouth. Because a fishes' top lip does not take any active part in respiration the fish is not overly affected by the hook. Hooking the fish top-lip also keeps it upright in the water, another contribution in keeping it relatively unharmed.

Cast gently, "underarm" preferably, slightly further than you want, then reel in line to suit. This helps to keep the pilot float near the bung and to keep the line under control. Leave the balearm off but make sure there is very little slack line out. When trotting the bung, assist the line off the reel and when the bung is approaching reel line in (the balearm being engaged for this). If possible keep the bung staright in front of you and walk along the bank to keep up with the current. The less line out the easier the strike will be.

Fish just beyond the weeds or any reeds and also try in slack water, under over hanging trees or on known deep holes.
