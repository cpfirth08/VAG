A Winter of Piking 1975/6

 Home

A record of Chris & Rit's piking trips during Winter of 1975/6. The location was the Carp Hole, unless indicated. Details are as reported, and therefore sometimes sketchy. Weekends or holidays grouped together.

There were 16 trips recorded during which we caught only 10 pike. If you doubt our determination take a look at Jan 2nd or Jan 24th or the weekend of March 6th!

Sunday, 7th December 1975

Runs: Chris 2 from under the willows, Rit none.

Method: Large pear bung, 18" trace, special bent back treble size 2.
